  Dataset metadata and its original author/funding/ethics fields are retained.
- **ARC demonstration repository:** neurolabusc/AphasiaRecoveryCohortDemo;
  BSD-2-Clause. The upstream notice is included as `ARC_DEMO_LICENSE.txt`.
  The original analysis follows its regional lesion-burden construction and
  uses the aligned public arterial label table.

  10.6084/m9.figshare.23579943.v1; CC0. The source is retrieved by the optional
  downloader; the frozen intersection and derived cross-check are supplied.

  own attribution or licensing terms.

  licenses. They are installed separately. Model weights are not redistributed
